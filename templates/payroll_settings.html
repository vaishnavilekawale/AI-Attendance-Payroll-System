Quick Guide
Automatic Payroll

Set the day and time for automatic payroll generation. The system will generate payroll for all active employees without manual intervention.

Professional Tax

Configure monthly professional tax amounts. These are deducted from employee salary before calculating net pay.

Company Details

These details appear on the professional payslip PDF sent to employees.

Important Notes
  • Payroll will not be generated if it already exists for the month
  • Payslips are saved in YYYY/MM format
  • Email requires SMTP configuration in .env
  • Scheduler runs automatically with Flask app
